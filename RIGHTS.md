# Rights and contribution policy

## What this release contains

This edition contains selected original interface and application code from the maintainer's privately developed website project, original CSS and SVG geometry, newly written fictional sample records, and independently authored historical game-guide summaries with source attribution. These original project contributions are released under the [MIT License](LICENSE). Third-party source works and game rights remain separate.

The catalogue's sample rods, baits, fish, places, prices, attributes and unlock conditions are invented demonstrations. They are not Fish It! gameplay data or a complete game inventory. Tool-usage walkthroughs explain the website. Separately labelled historical game guides retain original project-written summaries of named quests, routes and acquisition references, with sources, review dates, conflicts and uncertainties. They do not establish current game verification or tested gameplay advice.

The private project's commit history, research archives, downloaded source pages, raw source quotations, screenshots, game artwork, videos, account information and original bulk gameplay catalogue are excluded from this distribution. Original guide summaries may mention limited facts such as names, reported objective quantities or dated price disagreements; they do not reproduce full source tables or articles. The application does not automatically import or download excluded materials.

This document records the release's scope and contribution policy. It does not certify legal clearance or guarantee that a particular downstream use is permitted in every jurisdiction.

## Adding real data or media

If you build a game-specific edition, obtain and independently verify current information outside this distribution. Check the actual game, applicable source terms and relevant rights; do not rename fictional fixtures into purported game facts or represent sample outputs or dated guide summaries as verified current recommendations.

Contributions must:

- Be created by the contributor or accompanied by a documented license or permission that allows the proposed redistribution and modification.
- Identify any third-party material, its origin, applicable license, required attribution and changes. Keep its license separate where it is not MIT-compatible.
- Distinguish factual observations from copied prose, extracted table arrangements, screenshots, artwork and database contents. Reproducing an entire compilation requires its own review.
- For real game facts, record the source, observation date, relevant version and uncertainty. A link alone does not verify accuracy or grant reuse rights.
- Exclude credentials, private messages, personal paths, account identifiers, private research archives and unrelated project history.
- Avoid automatic source scraping or media downloads as part of installation, building or ordinary browsing.

Unsupported or unlicensed third-party media should remain absent. Use an original neutral placeholder instead of a copied image or a purported reconstruction of one.
